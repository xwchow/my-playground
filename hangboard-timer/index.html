Hangboard Protocols & Best Practices

Max Hangs

10s Hang · 3m Rest · 3-6 Sets

Pioneered by Dr. Eva López and Steve Maisch. Targets maximum neural recruitment and peak strength on a standard 18-20mm edge. Uses high added weight (or minimum edge) at 85-90% maximum intensity.

Goal: Peak force on cruxes.

7:3 Repeaters

7s On / 3s Off × 6 Reps · 3m Rest

Pioneered by the Anderson brothers (Rock Prodigy). Simulates climbing sequences with intermittent isometric contractions. Builds forearm muscular endurance and aerobic finger capacity.

Goal: Pump management & sustained crux blocks.

Density Hangs

30-45s Hang · 3m Rest · 3 Sets

Pioneered by Dr. Tyler Nelson and Keith Baar. Long duration sub-maximal hangs (bodyweight or slight assistance) that stimulate collagen synthesis and strengthen flexor tendon/pulley architecture.

Goal: Connective tissue health & injury resilience.

Safety & Protocol Hygiene

  • Always perform a comprehensive 15-20 min warm-up (pulse raiser, band work, progressive boulder problems) before loading edges.
  • Maintain engaged shoulders, active scaps, and a slight bend in the elbows. Never hang on passive, deadlocked joints.
  • Use strict Half Crimp (PIP joints at 90°, thumb uncrossed) or open-hand drag to protect A2/A4 pulleys.
  • Stop immediately if you feel sharp localized pain or strain in the flexor tendons or lumbricals.
