Step-by-Step Explanation

14 July 1789

Calculated using John Conway's Doomsday Rule

1 Century Anchor Sunday (0)

Century 1700s has anchor day Sunday.

2 Year Doomsday Wednesday (3)

For year 89: 89 / 12 = 7 (rem 5) + 5/4 (1) = 7 + 5 + 1 = 13 ≡ 6 mod 7. Century (0) + 6 = 6 (Saturday).

3 Month Anchor July 11 (7/11)

In July, the Doomsday anchor is July 11 (mnemonic: "7-Eleven").

4 Final Day Result Tuesday

Target is July 14: 14 − 11 = +3 days from Doomsday. Saturday (6) + 3 = 9 ≡ 2 mod 7 = Tuesday.

2 Year Offset Formula: The "Odd + 11" Method

Let T be the last two digits of the year:

1. Check Parity If T is odd, add 11 (make it even).
2. Halve It Divide by 2 (T ÷ 2).
3. Check Parity Again If the result is odd, add 11.
4. Distance to 7 Subtract from the next multiple of 7 (or 7 − rem).

Example for '89: 89 + 11 = 100 → 100 ÷ 2 = 50 → 50 is even → Next mult of 7 is 56 → 56 − 50 = +6 offset.

3 Month Anchors (Always Fall on Doomsday!)

Even Months (Match their number):
  • 4/4 (Apr 4), 6/6 (Jun 6)
  • 8/8 (Aug 8), 10/10 (Oct 10), 12/12 (Dec 12)
Odd Months (9-to-5 at 7-11):
  • 5/9 (May 9) & 9/5 (Sep 5) → "9 to 5"
  • 7/11 (Jul 11) & 11/7 (Nov 7) → "7-Eleven"
March & Pi Day:

March 14 ("Pi Day" 3.14) or March 0 (last day of Feb).

Jan & Feb (Leap Year Shift):

Common: Jan 3 / Feb 28
Leap Year: Jan 4 / Feb 29

Calendar Note: Conway's Doomsday Rule applies to the Gregorian Calendar (introduced in 1582 with leap centuries divisible by 400). Standard mental calculation tests evaluate all modern dates using standard Gregorian rules.
